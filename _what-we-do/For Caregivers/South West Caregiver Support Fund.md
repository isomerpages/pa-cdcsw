<p><strong>$1,000 </strong>to help caregivers with self-care and caregiving
expenses.</p>
<p>&nbsp;<strong>Eligibility Criteria</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Resident of South West District;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Applicant or care recipient must be Singaporean;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Per capita household income ≤ $1,500 or blue CHAS card holder;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Caregiver and care recipient must reside in the same household; and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Care recipient requires assistance with min. 3 activities of daily living
(ADLs): washing / feeding / dressing / toileting / mobility; OR</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Care recipient has a certified permanent disability: physical / visual
/ hearing / intellectual / autism / others.</p>
</li>
</ul>
