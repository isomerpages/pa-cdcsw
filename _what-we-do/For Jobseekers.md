<p><strong>Jobs Nearby @ CDC</strong> is a localised job matching initiative
that helps residents find employment opportunities near their home.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Resident jobseekers can approach Job Ambassadors, who are deployed across
the South West District, for personalised guidance and support. Through
the programme, residents can:&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Access job openings through <strong>District Job Banks</strong>&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Receive personalised job matching support and career advice from <strong>Job Ambassadors</strong>&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Connect with potential employers at <strong>Community Job Clinics</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
