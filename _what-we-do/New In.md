<p><strong>About CDC Vouchers Scheme 2026 (June)</strong>
</p>
<p>All Singaporean households will receive $500 in CDC Vouchers brought forward
for release from January 2027 to June 2026, to help with daily expenses.&nbsp;</p>
<p>This is in addition to the $300 CDC Vouchers given on 2 January 2026.
Therefore, all Singaporean households will receive a total of $800 CDC
Vouchers in 2026.</p>
<p>Starting 11 June 2026, you can visit&nbsp;<a href="https://go.gov.sg/cdcv" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">go.gov.sg/cdcv</a>&nbsp;to claim the $500
CDC Vouchers (June). The process for claiming and spending the vouchers
remains the same.</p>
<p>The CDC Vouchers Scheme 2026 (January) are valid till 31 December 2026,
while the CDC Vouchers Scheme 2026 (June) are valid till 31 December 2027.</p>
<p><a href="https://vouchers.cdc.gov.sg/residents/info/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Learn</a>&nbsp;how
you can claim your CDC Vouchers 2026 (June).</p>
